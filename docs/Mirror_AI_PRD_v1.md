Mirror AI – Product Requirements Document (PRD)
v1.0
Product Name
Mirror AI
Tagline
Talk to Your Future Self.
1. Vision
Mirror AI helps people make better life decisions by enabling meaningful conversations with a personalized
version of themselves from the future.
The goal is to reduce the gap between who a person is today and who they want to become.
Mirror is not a chatbot.
Mirror is a digital future-self companion.
2. Problem Statement
Many people struggle with:
• 
• 
• 
• 
• 
Lack of long-term thinking
Poor decision-making
Inconsistent habits
Lack of accountability
Difficulty staying connected to their goals
Most productivity tools track tasks.
Most AI tools answer questions.
Very few tools create an emotional connection with a person's future.
Users often know what they should do but fail to do it because their future self feels distant and abstract.

3. Solution
Mirror AI creates a personalized future version of the user.
The future self:
